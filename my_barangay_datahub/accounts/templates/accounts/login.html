Barangay DataHub System — Privacy & Data Policy

By creating an account in the Barangay DataHub System, you agree to the following terms:

1. Data Confidentiality

All resident and household data collected and stored in this system is strictly confidential. Users are prohibited from sharing, distributing, or using this data outside of official barangay functions.

2. Authorized Use

This system is for authorized barangay officials only. Unauthorized access or use of another user's credentials is strictly prohibited and may result in account termination.

3. Data Accuracy

Users are responsible for ensuring the accuracy and completeness of all data entered into the system. Deliberately entering false information is a violation of these terms.

4. Privacy

Personal data of residents is protected under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173). All data must be handled with care and used only for legitimate barangay governance purposes.

5. Account Responsibility

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials. Notify the system administrator immediately if you suspect unauthorized access.
